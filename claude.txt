  Mostly right, with a few corrections needed:

  1. DefaultPluginRegistry → NonNetDb via query_current_state() — Not quite.

  3. Main → Mirror — Correct. main.rs:488 creates the mirror, passing it both
  ovsdb and nonnet.
  4. NonNetDb → Mirror and OvsdbClient → Mirror — Correct. The mirror holds
  Arc<OvsdbClient> and Arc<NonNetDb> and queries both during
  refresh_full_tree().

    - /org/opdbus/v1/ovsdb/... — yes (line 98 + line 194)

  6. Missing: SchemaEngine — When built with grpc feature, the mirror also takes
   an Arc<SchemaEngine>, which isn't in your diagram.

  NonNetDb.
