  It's only "lightweight" compared to GNOME/KDE, not compared to a headless
  install. Should finish soon.

  On the dbus/OVS/container routing question — that's the architecture
  op-network/OpenFlow is designed for: op-dbus acts as the control plane, when a
   user container gets provisioned it calls into op-network's OpenFlow
  controller, which programs OVS flows on ovsbr0 to route traffic for that
  container. The container gets an OVS port, and the controller sets up
  match/action rules for its traffic — including policy routing to priv_xray for
   privacy network users. That's what the privacy_network.proto RPCs
  (ProvisionUser, ConfigurePacketRouting) are wiring up.
