The thesis

What rides every block

Identity vector x Mutation vector x Meaning vector

Identity and mutation prove who changed what, when, and on whose authority. Voyage embeddings provide semantic meaning so the evidence can be searched by similarity, not just keywords.

Identity

WireGuard peer rooted in Netmaker. Stable session identity for privacy or compliance mode.

Mutation

Meaning

Voyage vectors land in Qdrant so the system can find evidence like this, not just strings matching this.

Netmaker hardening pattern

A control plane with no public service port

netmaker, netmaker-mq, and netmaker-ui are treated as loopback/socket services. Incus proxy devices expose Unix sockets under /run/netmaker. wg-xray terminates and routes by SNI behind WireGuard identity.
