The one paragraph

A mesh you can trust, not just connect

Traffic flows through a hardened pipeline — decoy ingress, identity sled, Xray header injection, a per-hop interceptor — where every request is classified, routed, gated against machine-readable policy (OSCAL), and recorded into a tamper-evident hash-linked chain. An AI layer reasons over that verified history but cannot execute anything itself. Same codebase ships as a privacy edition or a compliance edition — one flag decides which world you're in.

How it works

One governance pattern, every layer

Make the wrong thing impossible first; gate what's left; record everything. Rust says code can't be wrong, schema says state can't be invalid, A.N.N.A. says action can't be unauthorized — the chain says it's all on the record.

What's on every block

Identity vector × Mutation vector

Two structured vectors ride every block — together they make "who changed what, when, on whose authority" a provable query, and they're exactly what A.N.N.A. gates on.

Identity vector — the WHO

session_id = Argon2(PSK, salt=WG_pubkey)

Derived from the Netmaker WireGuard key. Stable, deterministic, unforgeable. Privacy mode: the key alone. Compliance mode: a verified user.

Mutation vector — the WHAT

The mut class of the subid taxonomy: what kind of change, on what resource, under what OSCAL authority. Reads (obs) and changes (mut) are distinct — so you can prove nothing was altered.

block = (identity · mutation · payload# · timestamp · prev#) — cross-examination becomes exact.

The semantic memory

Voyage vectors → searchable meaning

Identity & mutation are provenance. Voyage vectors are meaning. Every block, doc, and transcript is embedded by voyage-code-3 into Qdrant — so the chain is searchable by similarity. This is the bottom pane when you cross-examine the agent.

Embed

voyage-code-3 turns each chunk into a vector. Code-aware — understands infra, not just prose.

Search

Vectors in Qdrant. "Show me peers like the one that misbehaved" — similarity, not keywords.

Rebuildable

A derived projection of the chain. Lose it → re-embed from the canonical blocks.

Voyage = "find things like this." Identity/mutation = "who did exactly this, allowed?" Meaning to reason, provenance to prove.
