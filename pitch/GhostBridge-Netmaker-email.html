The One Paragraph

A mesh you can trust, not just connect

GhostBridge turns a Netmaker mesh into a trusted backbone. Traffic moves through decoy ingress, identity sled, Xray header injection, a per-hop interceptor, schema validation, OSCAL policy gates, and a tamper-evident Snowball ledger.

An AI layer reasons over verified history, but it has no hands: no execution rights, no direct control, and no ability to bypass the governed layer. It recommends into schema. A.N.N.A. Scribe and the Compliance Engine decide what is allowed to become real state.

Netmaker Hardening Pattern

The part we want Netmaker to see

The reference pattern is a Netmaker control plane with no exposed service port: loopback-only services, Incus proxy devices, Unix sockets under /run/netmaker, and Xray routing by SNI behind WireGuard identity.

No NIC posture

netmaker, netmaker-mq, and netmaker-ui are treated as loopback/socket services, not public daemons.

Sockets not ports

The attack surface moves to socket files such as /run/netmaker/api.sock and ui.sock, behind the identity gate.

Xray routing

wg-xray terminates and routes traffic by domain to the correct internal socket-backed service.
