DNS Records for 3tched.com Mail Server
======================================

Replace `<PUBLIC_IPV4>` with your public IPv4 address.

1. A Record (Webmail Hostname)
------------------------------
Type: A
Name: mail
Value: <PUBLIC_IPV4>
Proxy: Proxied through Cloudflare
TTL: Auto

2. MX Record (Mail Exchange)
----------------------------
Keep the current authoritative MX that Cloudflare is already serving for the
zone. In the current live config that is a managed `_dc-mx...` hostname, not a
direct `mail.3tched.com` target.

3. SPF Record (Sender Policy Framework)
----------------------------------------
Type: TXT
Name: @
Value: v=spf1 ip4:<PUBLIC_IPV4> ~all
TTL: 300

4. DMARC Record
---------------
Type: TXT
Name: _dmarc
Value: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:admin@3tched.com; ruf=mailto:admin@3tched.com; fo=1
TTL: 300

5. DKIM Record
--------------
Preserve the live selectors already published in Cloudflare. Rotate them only
when you are intentionally rotating mail keys.

To inspect the current live selectors:
  dig TXT default._domainkey.3tched.com
  dig TXT neo1._domainkey.3tched.com
  dig TXT s202602._domainkey.3tched.com

Testing
-------
After DNS propagation (can take up to 48 hours, usually minutes):

1. Test MX record:
   dig MX 3tched.com

2. Test SPF:
   dig TXT 3tched.com

3. Send test email to:
   https://www.mail-tester.com/

4. Check DKIM/SPF/DMARC:
   https://mxtoolbox.com/
