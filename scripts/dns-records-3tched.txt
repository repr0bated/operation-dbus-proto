DNS Records for 3tched.com Mail Server
======================================

Replace <CONTAINER_IP> with your Incus container IP address
Replace <YOUR_IPV4> with your public IPv4 address (if different)

1. A Record (Mail Server)
--------------------------
Type: A
Name: mail
Value: <CONTAINER_IP>
TTL: 3600

2. MX Record (Mail Exchange)
-----------------------------
Type: MX
Name: @
Value: mail.3tched.com
Priority: 10
TTL: 3600

3. SPF Record (Sender Policy Framework)
----------------------------------------
Type: TXT
Name: @
Value: v=spf1 mx ~all
TTL: 3600

4. DMARC Record
---------------
Type: TXT
Name: _dmarc
Value: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:admin@3tched.com
TTL: 3600

5. DKIM Record
--------------
After Maddy generates DKIM keys, you'll get a record like:

Type: TXT
Name: default._domainkey
Value: (will be provided by Maddy after setup)
TTL: 3600

To get DKIM record after setup:
  incus exec mail-3tched -- maddyctl dkim show default 3tched.com

6. Optional: Webmail A Record
------------------------------
Type: A
Name: webmail
Value: <CONTAINER_IP>
TTL: 3600

Testing
-------
After DNS propagation (can take up to 48 hours, usually minutes):

1. Test MX record:
   dig MX 3tched.com

2. Test SPF:
   dig TXT 3tched.com

3. Send test email to:
   https://www.mail-tester.com/

4. Check DKIM/SPF/DMARC:
   https://mxtoolbox.com/
